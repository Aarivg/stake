How the math works. Post-money SAFEs follow the YC post-money SAFE: the holder owns purchase amount ÷ cap of the company measured just before the new money, after the pool top-up and every other conversion. Pre-money SAFEs and notes price off the pre-money capitalization including the pool increase but excluding other convertibles. Notes accrue simple interest. An uncapped MFN SAFE takes the lowest cap and best discount of SAFEs listed below it (issued later), otherwise it converts at the round price. The option pool top-up sits in the pre-money, so existing holders absorb it. In the waterfall, granted options are treated as exercised (strike prices ignored), the unallocated pool gets nothing, and SAFE holders receive shadow preferred with a preference equal to the amount they converted. Non-participating preferred converts to common whenever that pays more.

For modelling and learning, not legal or investment advice. Real documents vary; check the conversion mechanics in yours. Scenarios you save stay in your browser.
